A. Project Objective

Twelve would like to implement Oracle Cloud Supply Chain Management (SCM) modules to support
its key financial and supply chain processes with a focus on core functionalities, future readiness,
and a common design across the modules. Additionally, the scope will provide input into the updates
to the production Financials modules natively integrated with the new SCM modules, as well the
creation of a Reporting Strategy. In order to ensure successful implementation and adoption of the
system, Twelve would like to engage GoSaaS.

GoSaaS will also establish reporting strategies and document configurations for various modules.
