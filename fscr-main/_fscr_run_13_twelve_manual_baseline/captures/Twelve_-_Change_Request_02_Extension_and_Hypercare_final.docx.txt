To Statement of Work Oracle Cloud SCM Implementation #4 (“Project”)

This Change Order (“Change Order”) modifies the existing Statement of Work for Oracle Cloud SCM Implementation #4 (“SOW”) between Twelve (“Client”) and GoSaaS, Inc (“GoSaaS”). This Change Order shall be incorporated into and made part of the SOW as if fully set forth therein.

Unless expressly stated otherwise herein, the provisions, terms and expressions set out in the Master Subscription and License Agreement (the "Agreement) shall apply equally in the Change Order and the terms and expressions defined in Agreement shall bear the same meaning when used herein.

As part of this engagement, both the Client and GoSaaS agree on the following:
​

Inclusion in Scope
Project Go-Live Date Extension
     The approach is to extend all current resources through the following dates:
Project date will be updated to:
Go-Live Date - September 1, 2025
Final Project Date, inclusive of Hypercare consisting of 6 weeks of Post Go-Live Support through September and the September Month End Close through October 10, 2025.
 A reduced team will be planned to remain through Hypercare.

Finance Hypercare:
Modules: General Ledger, Accounts Receivable, Fixed Assets, Cash Management, Payables, and PPM.
Scope
GoSaaS to provide support for the above listed modules for six weeks through the September 2024 close.  
International Payables Bank Wire Transfers: Discovery / analysis and remediation of the existing international bank wire transfers.

Supply Chain Hypercare
Modules: Procurement, Self-Service Procurement, Inventory, Manufacturing, and Maintenance
Scope
GoSaaS to provide support for the above listed modules for six weeks through the September 2024 close.
