A. Project Objective
Implement Oracle Cloud SCM modules (core functionality, future readiness, common design across modules); provide input into updates to production
Financials modules natively integrated with the new SCM modules; create a Reporting Strategy; document configurations.

B. Scope - Oracle Cloud Modules & Process Scope (Product Management, Materials Management, Procurement; TBD updates to Financials incl. GL).
Documentation of Business Scenarios, Requirements, Process Flows, Key Design Decisions, Configuration Templates.
 1 Product Information Management: 1.1 items for maintainable equipment, assets, spare parts; 1.2 items for manufactured product; 1.3 enable manufacturing & maintenance structures
 2 Inventory and Cost Management: 2.1 inventoriable items (spares, bulk, VMI, manufactured), receiving vs PO, return to vendor, putaway;
   2.2 current Plant locations configured as Inventory Organizations; 2.3 Costing Organization Structure and Cost Books;
   2.4 costing for manufactured inventory; 2.5 costing for maintenance equipment; 2.6 costing for inventoried material
 3 Self-Service Procurement: 3.1 non-catalog requisitioning; 3.2 requisitioning of inventory materials for maintenance/manufacturing;
   3.3 PO delegation of authority (approvals); 3.4 project and non-project accounting rules for POs and receipts; 3.5 reuse Coupa configuration values/concepts where applicable
 4 Purchasing: 4.1 project and non-project requisitions and POs; 4.2 DOA of requisitions and POs
 5 Supplier Portal: 5.1 supplier registration; 5.2 P2P inquiry; 5.3 supplier invoice creation; 5.4 supplier self-maintenance; 5.5 supplier onboarding managed by Twelve
 6 Manufacturing: "ready to use" (structures, work definitions, work centers, work areas, operations); no structures/work definitions configured or migrated;
   process manufacturing only (discrete NOT in scope); manual release/complete of WOs, issue/receive; MES integration NOT in scope (future vision only)
 7 Maintenance: "ready to use"; no assets/equipment configured or migrated; equipment & structures, work definitions/centers/areas/operations,
   maintenance WOs, meters, maintenance programs (preventive), Installed Base trackable

B. Scope - Oracle Cloud Financials and Reporting
 In-scope Financials for remediation: GL, AR, Project Management, Grants Management, AP, Cash Management, Fixed Assets. Implementation of Supply Chain
 Sustainability and Expenses. Scope of production Financials updates "will be determined during the project".
 1 Project Cost Management: commitments for reqs & POs; PO receipts and AP invoices for project transactions
 2 Asset Management: updates to AP and Projects resulting from new SCM and Expenses modules
 3 Confirm Enterprise (Business Unit) Structure: add Requisitioning and Purchasing BUs into existing BU structure; centralized/decentralized/shared service
 4 Sustainability: data from AP supplier invoices and manual uploads; seeded reporting; EPM-enabled sustainability reporting NOT in scope
 5 Expenses: design session to confirm in/out of scope; if in: T&E, DOA for expense reports, project/non-project accounting rules
 6 Reporting Approach: OTBI / BIP / Fusion Data Intelligence / EPM usage; financial reporting for EPM Planning & Budgeting, month-end close, GL reporting
 7 General Ledger: support on accounting/journals from Inventory, Costing, Manufacturing, Maintenance, Purchasing, Expenses
