Phase 1
Moses Lake & PLM

Moses Lake Modules:
  *Procurement
  *Inventory
  *Maintenance
  *Order Management
Why?  With these Modules, R1 will start Day 1 Operations in Oracle.
​
Stacks:
  *PLM
Why?  BOM revision is critical when planning Stacks parts. It is also the first product to ‘become familiar’ with the PLM functionality in Oracle.
​
XFNL Teams:
  *Supply Chain
  *R1 Ops & Maintenance
  *Stacks TD

Basic Process Flows
Issue, Change & Receive Purchase Requisitions (for approval) and Purchase Orders
Receive and Track Inventory (by locations - including Stacks and Maintenance Parts)
Issue & Track Maintenance Work Orders (backlog reporting available)
Routing established; Align with BOMs; Picklists for inventory (for Maintenance Work Orders)
Issue and Receive Sales Orders (External & Internal Customers)

Roles & Responsibilities
MRO Buyer:  Issue POs, Vendor Management, Vendor Contracts
Inventory Management:  Receive/Track Inventory; Issue parts per picklists
Transportation Management:  Shipping for finished product, as well as product between sites; Issue/Receive Sales Orders
Maintenance Manager:  Creation and tracking of Maintenance Work Orders to completion
Maintenance Operators:  Check-in/-out; Upload photos/docs to Work Order completion

Execution Expectations
Create detail end to end process flow
Create Training Plan with SOPs, Videos & Use-Cases for UAT
Maintenance Items, SOPs and BOMs are uploaded
Routing/Steps identified per Maintenance BOMs
Customer Routing clarified and functionality enabled (i.e. Drop Ship vs Consignment)
Purchase Requisition Approval Process identified and training for end users

Out of Scope: Enhancements identified through the implementation?  *Add to a future scope.

We have had several sessions to discuss challenges/issues, as well as obtain end-user feedback.
Based on that feedback, we think we have an idea of what is needed for this implementation.

Scope:  The bare-essentials of what is needed to track and manufacture a product end to end from a back-office perspective.
Let’s take a look!

What is missing?  What is a bare-essentials that is not represented?  Are there any ‘nice to haves’ that might be ‘easy’ to incorporate?
After the session, a survey will be sent out to gather your thoughts and feedback that might have been missed.
