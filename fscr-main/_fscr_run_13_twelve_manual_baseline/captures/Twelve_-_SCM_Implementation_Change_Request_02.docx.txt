To Statement of Work Oracle Cloud SCM Implementation #4 (“Project”)

This Change Order (“Change Order”) modifies the existing Statement of Work for Oracle Cloud SCM Implementation #4 (“SOW”) between Twelve (“Client”) and GoSaaS, Inc (“GoSaaS”). This Change Order shall be incorporated into and made part of the SOW as if fully set forth therein.

Unless expressly stated otherwise herein, the provisions, terms and expressions set out in the Master Subscription and License Agreement (the "Agreement) shall apply equally in the Change Order and the terms and expressions defined in Agreement shall bear the same meaning when used herein.

As part of this engagement, both the Client and GoSaaS agree on the following:
​
Description of the Scope Change:
Inclusion in Scope
Project Go-Live Date Extension
The approach is to extend all current resources through the following dates:[a]
Project date will be updated to:
Go-Live Date - August 1, 2025
Final Project Date, inclusive of Hypercare consisting of 1 month of Post Go-Live Support through August and the August Month End Close through September 12, 2025.
 A reduced team will be planned to remain through Hypercare.
 

The scope for the Oracle Financials & Supply Chain modules for the AirPlant One Business Unit:
Modules: General Ledger, Accounts Receivable, Fixed Assets, Cash Management, Payables, Purchasing & Self-Service Purchasing
Scope
GoSaaS to take management of the above module scope and associated activities and deliverables by GoSaaS
The Twelve Benefit Corporation (001) and Twelve Rainier, LLC (003) (AirPlant One) legal entities, and the Twelve Operations and AirPlantOne Business Units.
Project scope includes Requirements, Configuration Management and Conference Room Pilot (Testing) activities and materials.
Supply Onboarding Strategy
The development of strategy for migrating or onboarding suppliers from Coupa into the Oracle Cloud.
Effort is included in the baseline effort.  
Execution of the strategy is out of this scope.
New RICE Scope
Focused on the retirement of all transactions in Coupa, the following are brought into scope:
The Function Design and execution for the conversion of Open Purchase Orders from Coupa into Oracle Cloud Purchasing.
Closed Purchase Orders will not be converted to Oracle and will be managed in a flat file. i.e. Excel spreadsheet.
The Functional Design and the execution for the conversion of On Hand Inventory Balances from Coupa to Oracle Inventory for the Stacks materials for the Alameda location.
Current inventory is Expensed with no planned impact on the income statement or balance sheet accounts.
International Payables Bank Wire Transfers
Discovery and analysis, and remediation of the existing international bank wire in integrations
