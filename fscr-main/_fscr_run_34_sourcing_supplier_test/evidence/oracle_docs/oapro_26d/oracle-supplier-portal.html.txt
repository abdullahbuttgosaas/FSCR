They can also access other related tasks in the Additional Links section. The Suppliers News section allows you to broadcast important announcements to all suppliers, while the Notifications section provides a glimpse of unread messages with a count and one-click navigation to review all notifications. Suppliers can access the following business objects using the navigation cards based on their function security from assigned job roles. Other business objects are planned for future updates. For the ones that are marked as Redwood, suppliers will navigate to the corresponding Redwood flows and not the classic flows (those with asterisk (*) requires feature or profile option enablement first).
