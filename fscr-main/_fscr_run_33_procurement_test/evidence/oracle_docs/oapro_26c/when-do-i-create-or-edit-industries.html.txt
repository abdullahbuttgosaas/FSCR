 Previous Next JavaScript must be enabled to correctly display this content Implementing Procurement When do I create or edit industries? When do I create or edit industries? To meet a specific business need, you may edit industry names or descriptions of industries except for those belonging to the North American Industry Classification System (NAICS). Edit the industry descriptions also to determine how they appear in an application. You may also create industries with specifications not included in the NAICS standards.
