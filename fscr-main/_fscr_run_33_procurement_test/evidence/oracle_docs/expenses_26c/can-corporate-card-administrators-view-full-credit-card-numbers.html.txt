Can corporate card administrators view full credit card numbers associated with tokenized card numbers in American Express? Yes. Tokenized card numbers are provided only for payment processing. American Express continues to display full card numbers, even if you receive tokenized card files. To view full card numbers with their associated tokens, you can request transmission of a GL1210 report to your AMEX mailbox.
