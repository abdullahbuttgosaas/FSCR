Settings That Affects Payment Options The following options available from the Payment Option choice list in the Create Company Account dialog box affect payment liability: Both , Company , and Individual . How Accounting Entries for Payment Options Are Calculated When an employee's expense report is exported to Payables, the amount of the payment request equals the amount due the employee.
